# 英语四六级 — 专项题库

## 听力专项

### 题 E1：短对话预判

听力材料：*W: I'd like to reserve a table for six at 7 pm tonight. M: I'm sorry, but we're fully booked until 9 pm.*

问题：What does the man imply?

**解答**：
1. 关键词：fully booked（订满了）
2. 女人要预定 7 点的 6 人桌，男的说 9 点前全满了
3. 暗示：7 点没有空位
4. **答案**：The woman cannot get a table at 7.

### 题 E2：长对话推理

听力材料：*M: I heard you're taking a gap year before graduate school. W: Yes, I'm going to teach English in a rural area. It's something I've always wanted to do. M: That sounds rewarding. But won't it delay your career? W: Actually, I think the experience will make me a better candidate for the job I want.*

问题：Why does the woman think the gap year is beneficial?

**解答**：
1. 关键句："the experience will make me a better candidate for the job I want"
2. 她认为支教经历会让她成为更好的求职者
3. **答案**：It will improve her job prospects.

### 题 E3：讲座理解

听力材料：*Today I want to discuss the impact of microplastics on marine ecosystems. Studies show that over 8 million tons of plastic enter our oceans annually. These plastics break down into tiny particles that are ingested by marine life, eventually entering the human food chain. Researchers at Stanford University recently found microplastics in 97% of tested seafood samples.*

问题：What is the main finding of the Stanford study?

**解答**：
1. 定位后半段"Researchers at Stanford University..."
2. 关键数字：97% of tested seafood samples
3. **答案**：Microplastics were found in 97% of seafood samples tested.

## 阅读专项

### 题 E4：选词填空

*The sharing economy has (1)\_\_\_\_\_ dramatically in recent years. Companies like Uber and Airbnb have become household names, offering (2)\_\_\_\_\_ to traditional services. However, critics argue that these platforms often (3)\_\_\_\_\_ existing regulations designed to protect consumers.*

**选项**：A) expanded  B) alternatives  C) bypass  D) declined  E) consumers  F) solutions

**解答**：
1. (1) 空前 "has" + 副词 → 需要过去分词。A) expanded ✅（大幅扩张）
2. (2) 空前 "offering" + 空后 "to" → 名词。B) alternatives ✅（提供替代方案）
3. (3) 空前 "platforms often" + 空后 "regulations" → 动词。C) bypass ✅（绕过监管）
4. **答案**：1-A, 2-B, 3-C

### 题 E5：长篇阅读匹配

段落 A: *The concept of "digital minimalism" has gained traction among young professionals. Instead of completely abandoning technology, proponents suggest intentionally curating which apps and platforms deserve their attention.*

段落 B: *A 2024 survey found that the average college student spends 4.5 hours daily on social media. This has been linked to increased anxiety, decreased attention span, and poorer academic performance.*

段落 C: *Some universities have introduced "digital wellness" programs, teaching students to set boundaries with technology while still benefiting from digital tools for learning.*

匹配题干：
1. The negative effects of excessive social media use
2. A balanced approach to technology usage
3. A campus initiative to promote healthy tech habits

**解答**：
1. 题干关键词 "negative effects" → 段落 B（anxiety, decreased attention, poorer performance）✅
2. 题干关键词 "balanced approach" → 段落 A（intentionally curating）✅
3. 题干关键词 "campus initiative" → 段落 C（universities introduced digital wellness）✅

### 题 E6：仔细阅读

*The gig economy has fundamentally altered how millions of people earn a living. While proponents praise the flexibility it offers, a growing body of research reveals significant downsides. A 2025 MIT study found that gig workers earn 30% less per hour than traditional employees performing similar tasks, after accounting for benefits and job-related expenses. Moreover, the lack of employer-provided health insurance forces many gig workers to either forgo coverage or purchase expensive individual plans. Despite these challenges, the gig economy continues to expand, driven by companies seeking to reduce labor costs and workers who value schedule flexibility above all else.*

问题 1：According to the 2025 MIT study, gig workers earn how much less than traditional employees?

问题 2：What is one reason gig workers struggle with health insurance?

**解答**：
1. 定位 "2025 MIT study found that gig workers earn 30% less per hour" → **答案：30% less per hour**。
2. 定位 "lack of employer-provided health insurance" → **答案：Employers do not provide health insurance for gig workers.**
3. 干扰项注意：文章也提到"buy expensive individual plans"，但不是"why struggle"的原因而是结果。

## 写作专项

### 题 E7：四级写作 — 议论文

题目：*On the Importance of Time Management*

**参考范文**：
> In today's fast-paced society, the ability to manage time effectively has become increasingly vital. College students, in particular, often find themselves juggling coursework, extracurricular activities, and social commitments.
>
> Effective time management brings numerous benefits. First and foremost, it enhances productivity. When we allocate specific time slots for each task, we accomplish more in less time. A case in point is the Pomodoro Technique, which many successful students use to maintain focus. Furthermore, good time management reduces stress. Knowing that we have planned adequately for deadlines gives us peace of mind.
>
> To improve time management skills, students can adopt several strategies. Making a daily to-do list, prioritizing tasks by urgency, and avoiding multitasking are all proven methods. Additionally, using digital tools such as calendar apps can help track commitments.
>
> From my perspective, time management is not merely a skill but a lifestyle choice. Those who master it tend to achieve more while maintaining a healthier work-life balance. Therefore, I strongly recommend that every student invest time in developing this essential ability.

**写作分析**：
- 引言：现象引入 + 观点
- 主体：两个论点（提高效率 + 减少压力），各配举例
- 结尾：总结 + 建议
- 亮点词汇：increasingly vital, allocate, enhance, peace of mind, merely a skill but a lifestyle choice

### 题 E8：六级写作 — 对比论述

题目：*Should College Students Be Required to Take Physical Education Courses?*

**参考范文**：
> The debate over mandatory physical education (PE) courses in universities has persisted for years. While some argue that PE courses detract from academic pursuits, I firmly believe they play an indispensable role in students' holistic development.
>
> On the one hand, opponents contend that college is primarily for academic and professional development. They argue that requiring PE courses consumes time that could be devoted to major-specific study or internship experience. From their perspective, students who wish to exercise can do so voluntarily.
>
> On the other hand, compelling evidence supports the case for mandatory PE. First, regular physical activity has been proven to enhance cognitive function. A study published in the Journal of American College Health found that students who exercised at least three times per week performed 15% better on exams. Second, PE courses combat the sedentary lifestyle prevalent among today's youth. With the rise of screen-based entertainment, structured physical activity is more necessary than ever.
>
> In my opinion, universities should maintain PE requirements while offering diverse options — from yoga to martial arts — to accommodate different interests. A healthy body is, after all, the foundation for a productive mind.

**写作分析**：
- 平衡论述（一边观点 + 另一边观点）
- 数据支撑（15% better on exams）
- 升级词汇：indispensable, holistic development, contend, compelling evidence, combat the sedentary lifestyle

## 翻译专项

### 题 E9：四级翻译 — 传统文化

原文：*春节是中国最重要的传统节日，家人会聚在一起吃年夜饭、放鞭炮和贴春联。*

**参考译文**：Spring Festival is the most important traditional festival in China, during which family members gather together to have a reunion dinner, set off firecrackers, and put up Spring Festival couplets.

**翻译要点**：
- "最重要的" → the most important（最高级）
- "家人聚在一起" → family members gather together
- 年夜饭 → reunion dinner（不是 "year-night meal"）
- 贴春联 → put up Spring Festival couplets

### 题 E10：六级翻译 — 社会话题

原文：*近年来，随着数字经济的蓬勃发展，越来越多的中国老年人开始使用智能手机进行网上购物、在线支付和视频通话。*

**参考译文**：In recent years, with the vigorous development of the digital economy, an increasing number of elderly people in China have begun to use smartphones for online shopping, digital payment, and video calls.

**翻译要点**：
- "随着..." → "With..." 或 "As..."（介词短语更简洁）
- "数字经济蓬勃发展" → vigorous/robust development of the digital economy
- "越来越多的" → an increasing number of（不用 more and more）
- 三个并列用 for 引导

### 题 E11：翻译易错题

原文：*应该采取措施保护环境，这是每个人的责任。*

**常见错误**：~~Should take measures to protect the environment.~~
**正确译文**：Measures should be taken to protect the environment, which is everyone's responsibility.

**分析**：中文无主语，英文必须用被动语态。"采取措施" = "Measures should be taken"。

### E20：选词填空—四级真题风格

*Online learning has become increasingly (1)\_\_\_\_\_ in higher education. Many universities now offer courses that students can (2)\_\_\_\_\_ remotely. 选项：A) popular  B) complete  C) flexibility  D) maintain  E) participation

### E21：仔细阅读—四级真题风格

*A new study published in the journal Nature Communications suggests that spending at least two hours per week in natural environments is associated with better physical and mental health. Researchers surveyed over 20,000 people across the UK about their weekly contact with nature — including parks, woodlands, and beaches — and their self-reported health status. The findings were striking: those who spent 120 minutes or more in nature per week were 59% more likely to report good health and 23% more likely to report high well-being compared to those who spent no time in nature. **解答**：最后一句 "benefits did not increase significantly beyond the two-hour mark" → **答案：Health benefits level off after two hours of nature exposure per week.**

### E29：四级高频近义词

判断每组近义词在用法上的区别：

1. **affect vs effect**
   - affect (v.) 影响：The weather affects my mood.
   - effect (n.) 效果：The medicine had no effect.
   - **记忆**：affect = 动作（动词），effect = 结果（名词）

2. **rise vs raise**
   - rise (vi.) 上升、上涨：The sun rises. Prices rose 5%.
   - raise (vt.) 提高、举起：raise your hand, raise prices
   - **记忆**：rise 不及物（自己升），raise 及物（被谁升）

3. **sensitive vs sensible**
   - sensitive 敏感的：sensitive skin, sensitive topic
   - sensible 明智的：a sensible decision
   - **记忆**：-tive = 有反应的，-ible = 可…的

# 近三年真题实战（2023-2024）

## 2023年12月 四级真题

### 真题 A1：翻译 — 中国人饮食变化

原文：*改革开放以来，中国人的饮食发生了显著变化。过去由于经济落后，人们只求"吃饱"，现在人们追求"吃好"。如今，低脂、低糖、高蛋白的健康食品越来越受欢迎。有机食品和绿色食品也进入了普通百姓的生活。*

**参考译文**：Since the reform and opening-up, the Chinese people's diet has undergone significant changes. In the past, due to economic backwardness, people only sought to "eat their fill", whereas now they pursue "eating well". Nowadays, healthy foods that are low in fat, low in sugar, and high in protein are becoming increasingly popular. Organic foods and green foods have also entered the lives of ordinary people.

**扣分点分析**：
- "改革开放以来" → Since the reform and opening-up（标准译法，背下来）
- "吃得饱 → 吃得好" → "eat their fill → eat well"（中文对偶，英文用对比）
- "低脂低糖" → low in fat, low in sugar（用 be low in 结构）

### 真题 A2：写作 — 校园活动报道

题目：*Directions: Suppose your school newspaper is inviting submissions about the most impressive campus activity. Write a short essay about it.*

**参考范文**：
> The most impressive campus activity I have ever participated in was the annual Charity Run organized by the Student Union last semester.
>
> The event attracted over 500 participants, including students, faculty members, and even the university president. We each pledged to run a certain distance, and sponsors donated money based on the total mileage covered. What impressed me most was not the amount of money raised — though it was substantial — but the overwhelming sense of community. Strangers cheered for one another, professors ran alongside students, and the collective energy was truly inspiring.
>
> This experience taught me that campus activities are more than just extracurricular events; they are opportunities to build connections, develop a sense of belonging, and contribute to something larger than ourselves. I believe such activities should be encouraged and expanded.

**评分亮点**：

### 真题 A4：选词填空 — 社交网络对青少年的影响
